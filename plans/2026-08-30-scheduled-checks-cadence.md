# Whether a generated repo wants the schedule this one refused

## Context

This plan collected three things that answer a question whose answer changes without any code
changing — `deps.audit`, the integration tier, and `ci.check-actions` — and asked whether they earn
a periodic run, since none of them is reached by anybody's commit.

**The repo-tasks half is settled and is no longer here.** Decided 2026-08-30 and recorded in
[`../contributing/quality-gate.md`](../contributing/quality-gate.md) under "Nothing here runs on a
schedule, and that is the decision": no scheduled workflow, `ci.status` as a deliberate pre-push
step, the other two run by hand when their subject changes, and the residual staleness risk accepted
rather than left pending. The reasoning was that a schedule surfaces staleness only to a reader, and
a repo pushing straight to `main` with no PR reviews has none — so an unwatched scheduled job would
look like coverage without being any.

What survives is the half that reasoning does not reach.

## The question that is left

A consumer generated by `scaffoldapy` inherits this repo's workflow templates and its gate, but not
its social shape. A repo with two contributors and a PR review habit **does** have a natural reader
for a red scheduled run, which is the exact premise the decision above turned on. So the answer may
legitimately invert per consumer, and nothing currently lets it.

## Open questions

[NEEDS CLARIFICATION: should `scaffoldapy` offer a scheduled audit workflow at all — as a copier
question, as a template a repo opts into later, or not at all? Against offering it: nothing ships
today, a generated repo can add a schedule in ten lines, and a question asked at generation time is
answered before the project has any idea whether it will have reviewers. For: the schedule is
exactly the thing nobody adds retroactively, and the concerns it covers (an advisory landing in a
quiet week, an action deprecating) are invisible until they bite.]

[NEEDS CLARIFICATION: does the integration tier run in CI at all? It needs a Docker daemon and pulls
`registry:3` plus a Debian base image. GitHub-hosted runners can do it, but it would be the first
thing in this family to need a daemon in CI and the cost is unmeasured. This is now only worth
answering if the question above says yes — measuring it is otherwise pricing something nobody
intends to buy. Carried from the retired repo-tasks half, where it was one of three items.]

## Recommended direction

Do nothing until `scaffoldapy` has a consumer that actually has reviewers. Until then the honest
answer is that this repo's accepted risk is also every current consumer's, because every current
consumer has the same single-contributor shape — and a copier question added now would be asked of
nobody who could answer it usefully.

If it is ever built, the daemon question decides its scope: the two daemon-free checks
(`deps.audit`, `ci.check-actions`) are the cheap version and can ship without ever settling the
integration tier's cost.
